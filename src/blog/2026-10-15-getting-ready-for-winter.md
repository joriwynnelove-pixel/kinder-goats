Every October we run through the same checklist before the rain sets in:

1. **Hoof trims** for everyone, since wet ground means softer hooves.
2. **Body condition checks.** Bred does get a little extra hay.
3. **Dry bedding** and draft-free shelters, with good airflow up high.
4. **Fresh water** that won't freeze. A heated bucket is worth every penny.

Maple, our herd matriarch, gets first pick of the warmest corner.
